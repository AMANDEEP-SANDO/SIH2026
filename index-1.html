Credit Schemes

Tailored Financial Products

Three specialized schemes designed to meet different needs.

🏪

Micro Finance Scheme

Up to ₹1.40 Lakh

For small self-employment projects and micro-enterprises.

  • Interest: 6.5% p.a.
  • Moratorium: 3 months
  • Repayment: up to 5 years
  • No collateral required
🏭

Term Loan Scheme

Up to ₹50.00 Lakh

For larger business expansion and capital-intensive projects.

  • Interest: 8% p.a.
  • Moratorium: 6-12 months
  • Repayment: up to 7 years
  • Margin money subsidy available
🎓

Educational Loan Scheme

Up to ₹10.00 Lakh

For higher education in India and abroad for SC students.

  • Interest: 6.5% p.a.
  • Moratorium: Course + 1 year
  • Repayment: up to 15 years
  • Full interest subsidy during study
Advanced Venture & Credit

Specialized MoSJE & MSME Initiatives

High-impact funding, equity, and subsidy programs for SC innovators, startups, and MSMEs.

🚀

ASIIM (Startup Incubation)

Up to ₹30.00 Lakh

Seed capital, mentoring, and incubation for SC youth innovators to transition to job-givers.

  • Funding: ₹10 Lakh/year milestone-based (Equity/OCPS/CCPS)
  • Interest: 0% (Grant/Equity investment, no debt)
  • Moratorium: 3 years incubation stage
  • Eligibility: SC/Divyang youth, TBI/AIC incubated, 51% SC equity
🏭

NSSH (MSME Subsidy)

25% Capital Subsidy

Ecosystem support for SC/ST MSMEs to scale, adopt quality standards, and fulfill public procurement.

  • Subsidy: 25% on term loans for plant/machinery (Max ₹25 Lakh)
  • Reimbursement: 80% on loan processing, PBG, and testing fees
  • Interest: 0% (Lowers overall bank borrowing costs)
  • Eligibility: SC/ST MSEs with Udyam Registration, 51% ownership
💼

VCF-SC (Venture Capital)

₹20 Lakh to ₹15 Crore

Concessional venture finance and equity capital to SC-owned commercial enterprises to scale operations.

  • Coverage: Up to 75% (≤₹5Cr) or 50% (>₹5Cr) of project cost
  • Interest: 8.00% p.a. (7.75% for SC Women/Divyang)
  • Moratorium: Up to 36 months on principal redemption
  • Eligibility: Pvt/Public Ltd, 51% SC shareholding for 6-12 months
🛡️

CEGSSC (Credit Guarantee)

₹15 Lakh to ₹5 Crore

Third-party credit guarantee cover to banks/NBFCs to sanction collateral-free debt to SC-promoted enterprises.

  • Guarantee Cover: 100% (up to ₹1Cr), 80% (₹1-2Cr), 70% (₹2-5Cr)
  • Interest: Bank benchmark (Collateral & risk-premium waived)
  • Moratorium: 6 to 24 months (principal only)
  • Eligibility: 51% SC shareholding for 6 months, no dilution during guarantee
🤝

NSFDC (Micro & Term Loan)

Up to ₹50.00 Lakh

Concessional credit to marginalized SC individuals and SHGs to set up micro-enterprises and self-employment units.

  • Term Loan: Up to ₹50L (90% funding) at 8.00% p.a.
  • Micro Credit (MCF): Up to ₹1.25L at 6.50% p.a.
  • Mahila Samriddhi (MSY): Up to ₹1.40L at 4.00% p.a. for SC women
  • Eligibility: SC caste certificate, Family income ≤ ₹3.00 Lakh, Age ≥ 18
